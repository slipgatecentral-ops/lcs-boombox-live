The next build pass

These are implementation directions for the next stage. The renders above are visual targets; the live scene has not been changed by this concept pass.

  1. Extend the existing TSL interior mapping

    Build on the facade’s recessed windows and room shading. Give close windows convincing room depth, varied curtains and blinds, with repeated instances sharing the same material.

  2. Keep glazing dirt and reflections distinct

    Localize dirt at frames and runoff paths. Vary pane roughness and tint while retaining broad reflections of the opposite buildings; avoid baking reflection shapes into the dirt texture.

  3. Build a rooftop silhouette kit

    Start with HVAC banks, ducts, service sheds, water tanks, telecom arrays and crane structures. Add supported cable spans and weathered sign frames, then reduce detail with distance.
